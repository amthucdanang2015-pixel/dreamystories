## Illustration style guide (all stories)

Premium hand-painted picture-book illustration: gouache and soft watercolour textures on warm paper, rounded friendly shapes, gentle rim light, subtle paper grain and a soft vignette. Calm, comforting, never frightening. Respectful, accurate Vietnamese settings and dress. Keep each character identical on every page (see the character sheet). No text, letters, logos, signatures or watermarks.

Every story then has its own **art direction** (below): a distinct setting, light, palette and signature motifs, so no two books share a look. Generic "storybook forest" backdrops are not used. Recommended size 1536×1024 (3:2) PNG for pages and 1024×1024 for covers; keep the focal subject in the central two-thirds, because the app crops covers to portrait shelf cards and landscape heroes.

Until a PNG exists, the app renders the story-specific vector scene from `src/components/illustrations/scenes/` for that cover/page, built from the same art direction. Dropping a PNG in place and re-running this script switches that page to the painting.

Each asset row has a short scene prompt; `assets/stories-manifest.json` also carries `fullPrompt` / `coverFullPrompt` with brand style + art direction + characters + scene combined, ready to paste into an image model.
